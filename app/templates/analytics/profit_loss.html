Financial Accounting Principles
  • Actual Batch Costs: COGS calculates the real unit purchase cost from the specific inventory batch sold, avoiding inaccurate uniform averages.
  • Wastage Isolation: Expired batches and broken goods are recorded into wastage losses rather than inflating COGS.
  • Return Deductions: Only resellable returned items replenish sellable inventory; customer refunds are deducted from revenue.
